Free for Android phones

It's an Android app: open ecec.dev/the-farther-we-stray/download on your phone to get it there.

On an Android phone

  1. On the phone, open this page and tap Download for Android.
  2. When it has downloaded, tap TheFartherWeStray-android.apk to open it.
  3. Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, and tap Install.
  4. Open The Farther We Stray from the home screen and hold the phone sideways.

It isn't on the Play Store, so Android might say it doesn't recognise the app. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

Updates: when a new version is out, download it from this page and install it over the top. Your beasts and saves stay.

The controls

  • The buttons sit at the sides of the screen, never over the game: the D-pad on the left, and A (select), B (back) and Start (menu) on the right.
  • Or just tap. In battle, tap a tile to aim and tap it again to act; drag to look around the board.
  • A highlighted button shows what it does, and your phone's back gesture works like B.

This is an early build. Battles, catching, breeding, the lab and the country map are in. The story is still being written.
