How to play

  1. Unzip the download somewhere like Documents or your Desktop.
  2. Open the DrawFight folder and double-click DrawFight.exe.
  3. Plug in a controller (Xbox pads work best), or use the keyboard.

Windows might say "Windows protected your PC". That's because this game isn't from a big company. Click More info, then Run anyway.

It keeps itself up to date. Each time it starts, it checks for new fighters and fixes and installs them by itself.
